# Kansei (感性)

> **感** (kan) = sense, feeling, emotion
> **性** (sei) = nature, quality, property
>
> The quality of feeling — sensibility as engineering discipline.

Perceptual engineering for digital craft. The construct that makes digital things feel alive.

## What Kansei Does

Kansei bridges the gap between "this looks correct" and "this feels alive." It covers:

- **Shader Engineering** — GLSL, R3F, WebGPU. Per-element material recipes.
- **Motion Physics** — Spring constants, perceptual timing, breathing quality.
- **Haptic Design** — Touch feedback vocabulary for ritual interactions.
- **Sound Design** — Auditory persistence, element frequency mapping.
- **Perceptual Timing** — Model Human Processor grounded UI choreography.
- **Material Simulation** — Internal volume, Urushi IOR, grain-as-perception.

## The Thesis

> Internal Volume, not Surface Diffraction.

Every existing holographic digital system is chrome — light reflects off the surface.
Kansei makes things amber — light travels through, hits warmth, returns with depth.

## Archetype: YANAGI

Sōri Yanagi (1915-2011). The butterfly stool. Teakettles that feel right.
Objects so well-designed they disappear — you don't notice the design, you feel the warmth.

Enriched by Miyamoto (the feel of Mario's jump), Hara (emptiness as material), Masuda (Kansei Engineering at Mazda).
